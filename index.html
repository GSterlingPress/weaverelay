PRIVACY FIRST

Look deeply.
Touch lightly.

01

Read-only diagnostics

The initial product is designed to inspect and explain—not change your infrastructure behind your back.

02

Minimum permission

We ask for the smallest practical access needed for each check and keep provider credentials server-side.

03

Redacted reports

Public-facing diagnostic results should not expose provider tokens, account IDs or other sensitive connection details.

04

No “Fix It” yet

Automatic repairs are intentionally outside the initial Early Access product. Diagnosis comes first.
